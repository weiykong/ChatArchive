# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 20, 2026**

ChatArchive is a browser extension that exports conversations from AI chat platforms into local files. This policy describes what data the extension accesses and how it is handled.

## Data collection

ChatArchive does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. No information leaves your browser.

## What the extension accesses

- **Page content** — The extension reads the conversation text on the active AI chat tab when you initiate an export. This content is processed locally and written to a file on your device or copied to your clipboard. It is never sent to any server.
- **Active tab URL** — The URL of the current tab is read to identify which AI platform you are on. It is not recorded or transmitted.
- **Local browser storage** — Your export preferences (format, filename template, message limit, scroll speed) are saved in your browser's local storage using the Chrome `storage` API. This data stays on your device and is never synced or shared.

## What the extension does not do

- No data is sent to any external server or third party.
- No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
- No user accounts or sign-in.
- No advertising.
- The extension does not access any tabs or pages other than the one you are actively viewing when you click Export or Copy.

## Remote code

ChatArchive does not load or execute any remote code. All extension logic is bundled locally.
